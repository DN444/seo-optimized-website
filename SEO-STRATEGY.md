# SEO launch and off-page plan

## On-page targeting

| Page | Primary keyword | Supporting intent |
|---|---|---|
| Home | school in XYZ City | best school in XYZ City, local school |
| Academics | school curriculum XYZ City | primary school, senior school |
| Admissions | school admissions XYZ City | apply to school XYZ City |
| Contact | XYZ Public School contact | book school visit |

Keep location, phone number and school name consistent everywhere online. Never repeat keywords unnaturally; write for parents first.

## Quality backlink plan

1. Claim and verify the Google Business Profile, Bing Places and Apple Business Connect listings. Add accurate NAP details (name, address, phone), school photos, opening hours and the website URL.
2. Request a link from the local education board, municipal directory, recognised school association and reputable local parenting directories.
3. Publish useful, original resources quarterly: enrolment guide, reading-at-home guide, student showcase and community-event recap. Offer these to local newspapers, partner NGOs and library/community websites as a resource link.
4. Ask partner organisations (sports clubs, arts groups, universities and charities) to link to a relevant event or partnership page—not generic paid directories.
5. Track referring domains and broken links in Google Search Console. Decline paid link schemes, link exchanges and low-quality bulk directories.

## Social media integration

- Replace each `#` social link with the school’s official Instagram, Facebook and YouTube URLs.
- Post short student-work highlights, events and admissions reminders, linking to the most relevant page (not always the homepage).
- Use a consistent school name, logo, location and website URL on every profile.

## Technical launch checklist

- [ ] Replace every placeholder domain and contact detail.
- [ ] Compress real images and give each one specific, useful alt text.
- [ ] Check every page on a phone and run Google PageSpeed Insights.
- [ ] Verify `https://your-domain/robots.txt` and `/sitemap.xml` after deployment.
- [ ] Add the site to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; submit the sitemap.
- [ ] Add Google Business Profile and use UTM-tagged links for social campaigns.
